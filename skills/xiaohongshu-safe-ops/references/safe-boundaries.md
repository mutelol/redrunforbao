# Safe Boundaries

## Operating model

Treat Xiaohongshu as a human-published channel supported by AI, not an autonomous distribution target.

Safe default:
- AI generates research summaries, angle options, titles, drafts, cover copy, tags, and review checklists.
- Human checks facts, adapts voice, adds real assets, and publishes manually.

## High-risk requests to avoid

Do not help with:
- automated login or session persistence using cookies or tokens
- direct publishing, scheduled posting through unofficial automation, or UI scripting against the live product
- bulk liking, following, commenting, messaging, or account farming
- bypassing risk controls, device checks, or bot detection
- fake testimonials, fake metrics, or fabricated user stories

## Safer alternatives

Redirect risky requests into one of these:
- generate a draft package for manual posting
- create a weekly editorial workflow
- build a reusable title library or hook library
- build a manual review checklist
- summarize post performance and propose new tests

## Content quality rules

Prefer:
- concrete screenshots, examples, and numbers
- first-hand experience and build logs
- narrow audiences and narrow promises
- helpful specifics over broad inspiration

Avoid:
- generic success slogans
- unsupported guarantees
- exaggerated claims of effortless growth
- repetitive AI phrasing and cliches

## Tone guidance

For Xiaohongshu-style content, aim for:
- useful and specific
- honest and grounded
- warm, plainspoken, and easy to skim
- vivid openings with low fluff
